# Human review rubric

Review anonymous MP4s before looking at blind-key.json. Score each dimension from
1 (missing/poor) to 5 (polished/intentional), using the same playback setup.

| CSV field | What to judge |
|---|---|
| concept | Distinctive visual idea and consistent art direction |
| story_or_dance | Developing story or expressive, varied choreography |
| motion | Deliberate movement, continuity and transitions |
| beat_sync | Visible actions match musical accents and phrasing |
| composition | Clear focal points, typography and visual hierarchy |
| ending | Purposeful conclusion that fits the excerpt |

Add timestamps in notes for specific strengths or defects. Review the soundtrack
and full video, not just stills. Look for distracting flashes, readability issues,
frozen sections and obvious render failures. The harness does not certify flash
safety. Empty scores mean not yet reviewed, not zero.

Report technical pass/fail, wall time and token use separately from human scores.
Do not crown a winner using model self-assessments. With repeats, report the
spread of scores and completion rate rather than only the best export.
